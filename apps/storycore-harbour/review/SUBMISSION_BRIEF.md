# StoryCore Harbour — Anna presentation brief

This is presentation copy, not a legal commitment or a submission authorization.

## Short description

StoryCore Harbour turns a concept, synopsis, or short script into a coherent visual-production package: a world bible, stable characters and locations, ordered scenes and shots, reusable generation prompts, continuity checks, saved project data, and a versioned JSON export.

## Value for Anna

- Demonstrates a meaningful Host-API-only creative workflow without requiring an Executa, GPU, local installation, provider key, or developer-paid inference backend.
- Produces a durable artifact rather than a transient chat answer.
- Uses Anna's LLM, App-scoped storage, window, permission, and quota boundaries directly.
- Supports English and French use cases across short film, advertisement, music video, documentary, comic/webtoon, and social video formats.
- Creates a natural bridge from accessible cloud ideation to optional advanced local production in StoryCore Desktop, while remaining useful on its own.

## Demonstration procedure

Follow `../demo/README.md`. The expected reviewer-visible flow takes less than five minutes after dependencies are installed:

1. submit the committed fictional concept;
2. inspect the world bible, character, and location;
3. inspect the scene and shot;
4. inspect continuity;
5. export and open the validated JSON.
